# Hello World

Welcome to my blog! After spending a lot of time setting up the technical foundation—from custom password protection plugins to layout refinements—it's finally time to share some actual content.

## What I Want to Share

This space will be dedicated to documenting my journey through the complex world of offensive security. I plan to dive deep into topics that fascinate me, including:

- **Malware Development:** Exploring the inner workings of loaders, obfuscation, and EDR bypass techniques.
- **Reverse Engineering:** Breaking down interesting samples and understanding how they tick.
- **CTF Writeups:** Sharing my thought process and solutions for challenging security puzzles.
- **Security Research:** Keeping track of new vulnerabilities and exploitation methods.

## Future Experiments

I'm constanty experimenting with new ideas. In the coming months, expect to see:

1.  **Custom Security Tools:** I'll be sharing some of the tools I build for my own research.
2.  **Deep Dives into Windows Internals:** Understanding the OS at a low level is key to mastering security.
3.  **Collaborative Projects:** Engaging with the community to learn and build together.

Thank you for being part of this first step. Stay tuned for deeper, more technical posts very soon!
